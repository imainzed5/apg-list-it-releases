Installing it

About a minute, once.

  1. Open the file

    When the download is done, open APGListIt-setup.exe from your Downloads.

  2. If Windows warns you

    Click More info, then Run anyway.

    Windows protected your PCMicrosoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting.
    More infoRun anyway

    It's our own app. Windows shows this for apps without a paid Microsoft certificate.

  3. Sign in

    Open APG List-It from the Start menu and sign in with the email and password you were given.

Had Alpha Listing Hub? Uninstall it first, in Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps. Your saved lists stay on your PC.
